# Project NERO Explainer Video Script

## Title

Project NERO: From Market Dashboard to Evidence-Driven Trading Intelligence

## Duration

About 60 seconds.

## Voiceover

Project NERO started as a simple market dashboard. Its first job was to read BTC, Gold, oil, and market news, then produce a research verdict.

Then NERO became live-aware. It connected to market data, news feeds, AI sentiment, and mobile alerts.

Next came accountability. NERO began recording paper trades, tracking predictions, checking outcomes, and asking the most important question: was the signal right or wrong?

Then NERO added intelligence layers: Historical Market Memory, White House impact analysis, ETF flow context, real yields, cross-asset drivers, cointegration, Granger causality, GARCH volatility, and dynamic beta.

After that, NERO became a Strategy TEST Lab. Multiple strategies now run in paper mode, each with its own win rate, expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, and P/L.

When a strategy performs badly, NERO does not ignore it. It quarantines the strategy, sends it to Strategy Doctor, and maps it to a repair candidate.

The current mission is simple: collect clean live-forward evidence, protect the trader from weak setups, repair failing strategies, and promote only what proves itself.

NERO is not a profit guarantee. It is an evidence engine. Its job is to reduce blind guessing and turn trading ideas into auditable data.

## Visual Flow

1. Birth: local Streamlit dashboard.
2. Live data: candles, news, sentiment, alerts.
3. Accountability: paper trades, prediction log, truth dashboard.
4. Intelligence: historical memory plus quant engines.
5. Test Lab: many strategies in parallel.
6. Quarantine: weak strategies are paused.
7. Strategy Doctor: repair candidates are tested.
8. Future: evidence-based promotion to stronger strategies.
